How to improve the implementation for the next year, 2026-2027

What we can do

Amplify RDM policy awareness

  • At the Data Steward team, we will continue to publish news items to inform and remind researchers about the RDM policy
  • Plan more visits to the research groups to present the policy and introduce their data Steward
  • We plan to organize a symposium about Research Cockpit to increase the awareness

Enhance training uptake

  • We have revamped the PROOF course on Open Science and RDM to include training on how to make DMPs
  • We have created RDM guidelines for PhD supervisors
  • We are preparing an e-learning course about Research Data Management

What you can do

You can help us by inviting us to your staff meetings. Please email rdmsupport@tue.nl or reach out to your data steward directly (Find your data steward).
